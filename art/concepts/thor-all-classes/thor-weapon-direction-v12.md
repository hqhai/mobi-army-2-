- Gunner: compact thunder pistol with hammer-like silver muzzle.
- Miss 6: long engraved Asgardian thunder rifle.
- Electrician: short runic electrical emitter.
- Rocketer: silver storm launcher with lightning chamber.
- Granos: heavy runic grenade launcher.
- Chicky: silver feather-shaped throwing weapon with lightning edges; retain chicken hood and wings.
- Tarzan: curved silver thunder boomerang.
- Apache: compact square-headed Mjolnir-style hammer, not a double axe.
- Magenta: silver Asgardian photon blaster.

Shared materials: silver metal, dark leather grip, Norse engraving and cyan-white lightning core. Red cape remains part of costume; no red Iron Man weapon parts in new lower-row designs. King Kong v9 electric banana equipment remains approved and unchanged.
